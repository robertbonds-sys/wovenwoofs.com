About

Woven Woofs is a daily series of embroidered patches — dogs in costumes, each one numbered, released one per day.

How they're made: each image starts as a Midjourney render, then gets picked, directed, and finished by Robert. The patches are digital artwork in an embroidered style.

You get a say in what comes next: vote on upcoming costumes, nominate breeds, or request your own dog as a future patch.
